⚔️ XP Hero Builder

Weapon Loadout

Click a slot to equip a weapon from your collection. Up to 6 weapons equipped at once, just like in-game.

Heroes

Add every hero you own, set their independent level / star grade / evolution, and mark which one is currently active.

Traits

Two trait groups, five synergy slots each (Fist / Vampire Fang / Dragon's Claw / Ogre Heart / Sage's Hourglass). Stack matching stat types across slots to unlock synergy-set bonuses.

Account Upgrades

Ability, Special, and Extra upgrade trees — permanent account-wide stat investment, independent of any one hero or weapon.

Farmable Items

Every material/currency item in the game, with real drop rates and exactly which boss or chest gives it. Click an item to see its sources, then "View Map" for where in the chapter progression it's found.

Monsters

Every enemy in the game, real portraits included. Filter by chapter or by boss/non-boss — bosses show their real title and, where confirmed, the exact stage they guard.

Build Guide

Pick one of your heroes to see build advice tailored to their current level, star grade, weapon loadout, traits, and upgrades.

Profile

Your account and cloud-synced builds. An account is entirely optional — everything else in this app already works fully without one, saved locally in your browser.
